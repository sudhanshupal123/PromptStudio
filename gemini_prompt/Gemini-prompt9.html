Photography of a man standing frozen in time amidst a rushing crowd. High-speed zoom blur effect. The crowd is a massive blur of motion streaks radiating from the center. The man in the center is perfectly sharp and focused, wearing a red coat. Long exposure style, desaturated cool tones, moody atmosphere, high contrast between the stillness of the subject and the motion of the crowd. make sure you keep these specific keywords: "Radial Motion Blur" or "Zoom Blur": This creates the effect where the blur looks like it is exploding outward from the center, rather than just moving left-to-right. "Razor- Sharp Focus": This ensures the Al understands the subject must not be blurry. "Frozen in Time": This helps the Al understand the concept of one person standing still while the world moves fast. "Indistinguishable Crowd": This ensures the background people don't have faces its white faces, keeping the focus on the main man.
